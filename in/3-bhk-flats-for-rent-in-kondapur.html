Kondapur: quick answers

What is the price per sq.ft in Kondapur?

Apartments in Kondapur are indicatively priced between ₹6,800 and ₹8,800 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Kondapur?

A typical 2BHK in Kondapur rents for about ₹28,000–₹40,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Kondapur good for investment?

Kondapur shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3.3% with a steady growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.
