      FAQ

      Questions HR teams ask.

      Who talks to the employee?

      A named Keetown coordinator, on WhatsApp and phone, from the first call until the employee is settled. HR sees every update in the portal.

      Can the employee pay and get reimbursed?

      Yes. Choose "company pays Keetown" or "employee pays, company reimburses" per move. Rent and deposits are always paid directly to the owner.

      Do you handle moves into Hyderabad from abroad?

      Yes. Video viewings, e-signed agreements and temporary stay mean employees can land with a home already lined up.

      What do you check before an office lease?

      Title and ownership, occupancy certificate, fire NOC, power load and backup, parking ratio, lock-in, escalation, CAM charges, rent-free fit-out period and exit clauses.

      Is employee data safe?

      We collect only what the move needs. Each employee sees only their own case; HR sees only their company's moves.
