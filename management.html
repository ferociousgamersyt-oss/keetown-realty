Compliance checklist

Hyderabad rules we keep on your side.

Regulations and rates change. Our team confirms current requirements with your advocate or CA before acting — this is a working checklist, not legal advice.

  • Written rental agreement with rent, deposit, escalation, lock-in and exit terms — stamped and, where needed, registered.
  • Tenancy law awareness — we work within Telangana’s rent-control framework and the Model Tenancy Act direction, and keep notices procedural.
  • Tenant verification — ID, employer proof and police-verification support.
  • GHMC property tax — dues tracked and paid before the penalty window.
  • Utilities — HMWSSB water and electricity bills tracked; no ghost dues at hand-over.
  • Income-tax readiness — rent ledger, TDS tracking for NRIs and a statement your CA can file from.
  • Society / RWA norms — dues, NOC, parking and move-in rules cleared up front.
  • Deposit hygiene — dated condition report and photo evidence for fair refund decisions.

FAQ

Owner questions, answered.

Do I need to be in Hyderabad?

No. Many of our owners live abroad. Agreements are e-signed, inspections are video-led, and rent goes straight to your account.

Who approves repair costs?

You do. Anything above a limit you set gets a quote and photos first. Emergencies like active leaks are handled immediately and reported.

What if my tenant stops paying?

We follow a documented escalation: reminders, written notice, negotiation, and referral to your advocate for legal steps under the agreement and applicable law.

How long is a typical agreement?

Hyderabad homes are commonly let on 11-month terms with an annual escalation. We will advise what suits your property and tenant.

Can you manage a plot or an under-construction unit?

Yes — via our Plot & land watch and handover-readiness add-ons.
