LB Nagar: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in LB Nagar?

Apartments in LB Nagar are indicatively priced between ₹5,200 and ₹6,800 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in LB Nagar?

A typical 2BHK in LB Nagar rents for about ₹17,000–₹25,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is LB Nagar good for investment?

LB Nagar shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3.3% with a steady growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.

How well connected is LB Nagar?
