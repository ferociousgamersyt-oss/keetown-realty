Bachupally: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Bachupally?

Apartments in Bachupally are indicatively priced between ₹5,200 and ₹6,500 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.
