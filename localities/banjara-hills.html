Banjara Hills: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Banjara Hills?

Apartments in Banjara Hills are indicatively priced between ₹11,000 and ₹20,000 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Banjara Hills?

A typical 2BHK in Banjara Hills rents for about ₹50,000–₹90,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Banjara Hills good for investment?

Banjara Hills shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 2.3% with a stable growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.
