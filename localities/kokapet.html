Kokapet: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Kokapet?

Apartments in Kokapet are indicatively priced between ₹8,500 and ₹12,500 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Kokapet?

A typical 2BHK in Kokapet rents for about ₹38,000–₹55,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Kokapet good for investment?

Kokapet shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 2.8% with a very high growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.
