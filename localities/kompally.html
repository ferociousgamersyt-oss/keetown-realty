Kompally: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Kompally?

Apartments in Kompally are indicatively priced between ₹5,500 and ₹7,200 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Kompally?

A typical 2BHK in Kompally rents for about ₹18,000–₹28,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Kompally good for investment?

Kompally shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3.1% with a high growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.
