Financial District: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Financial District?

Apartments in Financial District are indicatively priced between ₹7,500 and ₹10,500 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Financial District?

A typical 2BHK in Financial District rents for about ₹35,000–₹50,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Financial District good for investment?

Financial District shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3% with a high growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.

How well connected is Financial District?

Key connectivity: ORR + Nanakramguda. Walk-to-work for global banks and tech campuses. Large gated communities.
