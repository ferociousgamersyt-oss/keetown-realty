Shamshabad: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Shamshabad?

Apartments in Shamshabad are indicatively priced between ₹4,500 and ₹7,000 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Shamshabad?

A typical 2BHK in Shamshabad rents for about ₹14,000–₹22,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Shamshabad good for investment?

Shamshabad shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3% with a high growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.
