Uppal: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Uppal?

Apartments in Uppal are indicatively priced between ₹5,000 and ₹6,500 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Uppal?

A typical 2BHK in Uppal rents for about ₹16,000–₹24,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Uppal good for investment?

Uppal shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3.4% with a steady growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.

How well connected is Uppal?

Key connectivity: Uppal Metro. Eastern value corridor with metro and the Warangal highway link.
