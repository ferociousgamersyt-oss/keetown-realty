Narsingi: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Narsingi?

Apartments in Narsingi are indicatively priced between ₹6,500 and ₹8,500 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Narsingi?

A typical 2BHK in Narsingi rents for about ₹28,000–₹40,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Narsingi good for investment?

Narsingi shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3% with a high growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.
