Miyapur: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Miyapur?

Apartments in Miyapur are indicatively priced between ₹5,200 and ₹6,500 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Miyapur?

A typical 2BHK in Miyapur rents for about ₹20,000–₹28,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Miyapur good for investment?

Miyapur shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3.4% with a steady growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.

How well connected is Miyapur?

Key connectivity: Miyapur Metro (terminus). Metro-connected value zone for first-time buyers and tenants.
