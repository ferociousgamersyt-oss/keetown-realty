Gachibowli: common questions

What is the price per sq.ft in Gachibowli?

Apartments in Gachibowli are indicatively priced between ₹7,500 and ₹10,000 per sq.ft. Project, floor, facing and possession date move the price, so ask Keetown for live rates on a specific project.

How much is a 2BHK rent in Gachibowli?

A typical 2BHK in Gachibowli rents for about ₹32,000–₹48,000 a month, depending on furnishing, building age and the gated community.

Is Gachibowli good for investment?

Gachibowli shows an indicative gross rental yield of about 3.2% with a steady growth outlook. Like most of Hyderabad, returns are mostly appreciation-led, so treat rent as your carrying-cost offset.

Key connectivity: Raidurg line (nearby). Established IT hub with ISB, universities and deep rental demand.
