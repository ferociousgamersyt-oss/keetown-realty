## The button and the landing page
- Meta: CTA button **Learn more** (or **Get quote**). If you prefer people to stay inside Instagram, use an **Instant Form** with the same questions as the website form (need, name, mobile).
- Destination URL (website form opens by itself):
  `https://keetown-realty.pages.dev/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=one_team#interested`
  Change `utm_source` per platform (facebook, youtube, linkedin). To pre-select a service add `&interest=rent|buy|manage|sell|build|relocate|community`.
- Every form sent becomes a lead in Keetown OS with the campaign tags. If you add the Meta Pixel or Google tag, a `Lead` / `generate_lead` event fires automatically.
