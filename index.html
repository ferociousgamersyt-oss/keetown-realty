Questions people ask

What does Keetown Realty do?

We help tenants rent verified homes, buyers purchase with legal checks before any token, sellers find serious buyers, owners and NRIs with complete property management, builders sell inventory, gated communities run an exclusive rental and resale desk, and companies relocate employees and find office space, all in Hyderabad.

Which areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

Kokapet, Financial District, Gachibowli, Madhapur / HITEC City, Kondapur, Narsingi, Manikonda, Tellapur, Miyapur, Bachupally, Kukatpally, Kompally, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Uppal, LB Nagar and Shamshabad.

Is browsing properties free?

Yes. Browsing, saving homes and getting matches is free. Any service fee is explained and agreed before you commit.

How are owners and tenants verified?

Everyone signs in with their mobile number and a one-time password, and tenants, owners and sellers verify their identity with DigiLocker. We keep only the verified name and status.

Do you manage properties for NRIs?

Yes. Tenant placement, rent collection, repairs, GHMC tax and bills, with video inspections and regular statements, so you can own property in Hyderabad from anywhere.

How do I get started?

Tap “I’m interested”, tell us what you need, and we call you back within a working day, or WhatsApp us on +91 63017 78084.
